# Repository Governance

Bridge Node 7 is the maintainer and release authority for this repository.

Public changes require evidence of need, bounded scope, tests, documentation,
public-boundary review, and human approval. Schema-breaking changes require a
major version. Releases require a clean tree, passing local and hosted checks,
a reviewed commit, signed tag, deterministic archive, checksum verification,
and recorded limitations.

Automation may validate structure and declared conditions. It may not certify
truth, qualify a source, authorize release, or replace human judgment.
