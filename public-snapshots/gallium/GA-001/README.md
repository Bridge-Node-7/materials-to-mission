# GA-001 — Gallium Public-Source Evidence Snapshot

**Snapshot version:** 1.0.0  
**Review date:** August 10, 2026  
**Kind:** public-source evidence snapshot  
**Public maturity:** M0

GA-001 demonstrates the Materials-to-Mission method against bounded public evidence. It is
not a real operational Case 001, supplier assessment, qualification decision, acquisition
decision, mission-readiness claim, or certification.

## Scope

The snapshot covers four questions:

1. Is gallium publicly identified as a critical material/mineral with semiconductor relevance?
2. What does current public U.S. evidence say about domestic production and import reliance?
3. What public evidence exists for current domestic-recovery activity?
4. Which Materials-to-Mission pathway links remain unproven by the reviewed public evidence?

The result deliberately leaves qualification, acquisition access, program-specific validation,
and specific mission availability **unknown**.

## Source Standard

GA-001 uses only official U.S. Government sources for v1.0.0:

- U.S. Geological Survey / National Minerals Information Center
- U.S. Geological Survey final 2025 critical-minerals-list publication
- U.S. Department of Energy TRACE-Ga publications

The repository stores source metadata and original paraphrase only. It does not redistribute
the source webpages, PDFs, images, or long quotations.

## Interpretation Boundary

Public evidence supports a visible gallium supply-dependence condition and active domestic
recovery work. It does not establish that the resulting material is currently qualified,
available to a particular acquisition path, or ready for a particular mission.

**Unknown is not equivalent to favorable. Evidence informs. Humans decide. Responsibility remains.**
