# Code of Conduct

Participation must remain respectful, professional, evidence-aware, and safe.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of protected
information, fabricated evidence, and unsupported claims of certification or
qualification are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately through the project security or Strategic
Inquiry channels. Maintainers may edit, reject, lock, or remove contributions
that violate these standards or the public-information boundary.
