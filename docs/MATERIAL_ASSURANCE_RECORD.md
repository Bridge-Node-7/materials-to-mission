# Material Assurance Record

The Material Assurance Record, abbreviated MAR, is the complete evidence-bearing
decision-time record for a bounded decision.

In v0.1.0 it includes mission and dependency context, material and source
identity, process and supplier pathway, requirements, evidence, samples and lots
where applicable, tests, results, uncertainty, risks, weak links, alternatives,
critical conditions, mitigations, limitations, rights boundaries, reviews,
proposed disposition, next action, success signal, stop rule, and reassessment
trigger.

The v0.1.0 MAR does not encode a post-decision observed-outcome record. After an
accountable human acts, later proof, observed outcomes, and institutional
learning belong in separate governed records that preserve a link to the
original decision lineage. The MAR and Decision Passport remain historical
decision-time snapshots; they are not silently rewritten after reality arrives.

The MAR is not a marketing summary. It is the traceable record from which the
Decision Passport is produced.
