# Material Assurance Record

The Material Assurance Record, abbreviated MAR, is the complete evidence-bearing
record for a bounded decision.

It includes mission and dependency context, material and source identity,
process and supplier pathway, requirements, evidence, samples and lots where
applicable, tests, results, uncertainty, risks, weak links, alternatives,
critical conditions, mitigations, limitations, rights boundaries, reviews,
disposition, action, outcome, proof, and reassessment.

The MAR is not a marketing summary. It is the traceable record from which the
Decision Passport is produced.
