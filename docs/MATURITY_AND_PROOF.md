# Maturity and Proof

## M0 — Experimental Public Method

Public method, schemas, CLI, synthetic fixtures, validation, release evidence, and explicit
limitations. M0 may be published before Case 001.

## M1 — Real Workflow Proof

One approved real Case 001, applicable evidence, Material Assurance Record, independent
review, human disposition, action, and measured outcome.

## M2 — External and Commercial Proof

- P3: qualified independent review
- P4: intended-user application or formal evaluation
- P5: signed, paid or sponsored, delivered, reviewed, and accepted engagement
- P6: second accepted delivery that materially reuses the method

## M3 — Integrated Experimental Product

Case-derived interface behavior, released interoperability contract, complete product V&V,
and controlled public integration.

## M4 — Stable Institutional Product

Repeated materially different cases, stable versus case-specific requirements, P5 and P6,
continuing assurance, sustainable security and maintenance, and repeatable economics.

The public v0.1.0 repository is M0 only.
