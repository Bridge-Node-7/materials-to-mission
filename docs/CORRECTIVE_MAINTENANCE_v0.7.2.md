# v0.7.2 Maintenance Summary

v0.7.2 improved the public Materials-to-Mission experience without changing evidence, maturity, or decision authority.

## User-facing improvements

- Narrow-screen material details use native dialog behavior with reliable close and focus return.
- Dialog naming remains valid throughout the interaction lifecycle.
- The public orientation explains the controlled 15-count rare-earth convention without reclassifying the dataset.
- Editable-install diagnostics provide clearer recovery guidance.

## Preserved boundaries

- GA-001, MF-001, and YIG-001 evidence remained unchanged.
- M0 remained M0.
- Human consequential authority remained explicit.
- No qualification, certification, acquisition approval, mission readiness, supplier validation, customer validation, or autonomous decision claim was added.

## Known limitations

Physical-device mobile UAT and human assistive-technology UAT were not separately attested for this maintenance release. Anti-framing remains limited by the hosting layer.
