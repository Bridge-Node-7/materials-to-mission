The public experience includes:

- the USGS 2025 60-mineral Atlas field;
- two deeper public examples: Gallium and Yttrium Iron Garnet (YIG);
- progressive evidence disclosure through connections, material systems, evidence basis, pathway, and proof;
- visible unknowns and Evidence Horizon;

Gallium is a reviewed public-source pathway. YIG is a reviewed engineered-material-system context. Reviewed does not mean qualified.
