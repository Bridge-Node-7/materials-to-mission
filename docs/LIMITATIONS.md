# Limitations

Version `0.1.0` is an experimental public baseline.

- Only synthetic examples are included.
- The evidence model has not been stabilized across multiple real cases.
- Public-boundary scanning cannot identify every sensitive fact.
- JSON Schema validation cannot determine truth or applicability.
- The report generator does not replace independent technical, legal, security, or commercial review.
- No certification, qualification, compliance, capacity, customer, or government claim is made.
