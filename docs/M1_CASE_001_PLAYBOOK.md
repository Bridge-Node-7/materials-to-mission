# M1 Real Case 001 Playbook

## Status

This playbook prepares one protected case. It does not authorize a case or claim M1.

## Select the decision

Choose one consequential decision that is bounded, lawful to handle privately, and capable
of producing a measurable outcome.

Record the decision statement, intended user, named human decision owner, disposition
authority, deadline, scope, reversibility, and stop conditions.

## Approve handling before evidence intake

Approve contracting and data authority, confidentiality, intellectual-property and
publication boundaries, export-control and restricted-information screening, the private
system of record, access and retention, approved AI use, independent review, and final
release authority.

## Build the record

Complete the Decision Charter, source-linked Evidence Records, Material Assurance Record,
Decision Passport, evidence inventory, contradiction record, uncertainty record, and
independent-review record.

Unknown, unsupported, contradicted, expired, revoked, superseded, and partially supported
evidence remains visible.

## Review, decide, act, and prove

The independent reviewer examines applicability, contrary evidence, assumptions, critical
conditions, the governing weak link, alternatives, action requirements, and limitations.

The named authority records the disposition, rationale, accepted limitations, authorized
action, owner, due date, acceptance criteria, stop rule, and escalation path.

A mitigation plan is not closure evidence. Closure requires the named authority, required
evidence, and a recorded closure decision.

## Measure outcome and reassess

Keep evidence quality, decision speed, action completion, intended-user acceptance,
reviewer acceptance, outcome, rework, and reassessment triggers separate. Do not produce an
automatic approval score or universal ROI score.

## Publication

Publish nothing by default. A sanitized derivative requires written authorization,
technical review, confidentiality review, IP review, security and export review,
public-boundary scanning, documented limitations, and final human release approval.

## M1 acceptance

M1 may be claimed only after one approved protected case has applicable evidence, complete
records, independent review, a named human disposition, an authorized executed action, a
measured outcome, no public-boundary breach, and a reassessment trigger.
