# AI Provenance

AI may assist with retrieval, extraction, comparison, organization, drafting,
missing-field detection, schema validation, and review preparation.

AI may not autonomously certify compliance, determine legal ownership or origin,
qualify a source, accept a corrective action, close a critical risk, authorize
publication, or make the consequential decision.

Records disclose whether AI was used, which tasks it supported, which underlying
sources were used, and who reviewed the output.
