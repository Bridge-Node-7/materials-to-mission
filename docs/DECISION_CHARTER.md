# Decision Charter

The Decision Charter freezes the problem before analysis begins.

Required elements include the decision statement, mission, scope, stakeholder
role, human decision owner, disposition authority, deadline, consequences,
requirements, acceptance criteria, stop conditions, classification, allowed AI
use, reviewers, and required outputs.

A charter is ready only when the accountable decision owner agrees that the
record addresses a real decision and that the evidence standard is appropriate.
The public template is located at `templates/decision-charter.template.json`.
