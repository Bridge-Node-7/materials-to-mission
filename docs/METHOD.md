# Method

## Purpose

Materials-to-Mission connects evidence about materials and supply pathways to a
human decision. It is a method for reducing uncertainty, not an automated
qualification authority.

## Operating Sequence

1. **Define** the mission, decision, scope, authority, deadline, and consequence.
2. **Trace** material, source, process, supplier, facility, component, and system dependencies.
3. **Classify** evidence by basis and claim state.
4. **Expose** risks, governing weak links, contradictions, expiry, and unknowns.
5. **Compare** at least two options unless a documented exception applies.
6. **Apply** noncompensating critical conditions.
7. **Decide** through an accountable human authority.
8. **Act** with acceptance criteria, success signals, stop rules, and reassessment triggers.
9. **Prove** the result and preserve the decision lineage.

## Allowed Dispositions

- `ADVANCE`
- `VALIDATE`
- `PARTNER`
- `HOLD`
- `STOP`

A triggered critical condition blocks `ADVANCE` and `PARTNER` unless the
condition is resolved and the record is reassessed.
