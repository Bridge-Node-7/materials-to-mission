# M1 Readiness

## Status

Materials-to-Mission is currently **M0 only**. This record defines the minimum evidence
needed to begin and accept M1 work. It is not an M1 completion claim and does not authorize
a real case, public disclosure, qualification, certification, or deployment.

## M1 Definition

M1 means one approved real Case 001 with applicable evidence, a complete Material
Assurance Record, independent review, a named human disposition, an authorized action,
a measured outcome, and a reassessment trigger.

## Entry Conditions for Case 001

Before real case work begins, the responsible authority must document:

1. the bounded decision and named human decision owner;
2. the legal, contractual, intellectual-property, export-control, privacy, and disclosure
   authority for the work;
3. the private system of record and access controls;
4. the applicable requirements and noncompensating critical conditions;
5. the evidence plan, source rules, expiration rules, and contradiction treatment;
6. the independent reviewer and review scope;
7. the authorized actions, stop conditions, reversibility, and escalation path;
8. the intended outcome measures and reassessment schedule.

## Required M1 Evidence Package

A complete private Case 001 package must include:

- an approved Decision Charter;
- source-linked Evidence Records;
- a complete Material Assurance Record;
- a Decision Passport derived from the full record;
- documented unknowns, contradictions, expiration, and unsupported claims;
- an independent review record;
- the named human disposition and rationale;
- the authorized action and execution record;
- measured outcome evidence;
- the reassessment trigger and responsible owner.

## Acceptance Conditions

M1 may be claimed only when all required records are complete, internally consistent,
reviewed by the named authorities, and supported by the measured outcome. A successful
schema validation or generated Decision Passport is necessary tooling evidence, not proof
that the real-world conclusion is correct.

## Public Boundary

Real Case 001 records remain private unless a separate disclosure review authorizes a
sanitized public derivative. The public repository must not receive real supplier,
customer, laboratory, sample, lot, facility, price, capacity, vulnerability, restricted,
classified, controlled unclassified, export-controlled, or patent-sensitive information.
