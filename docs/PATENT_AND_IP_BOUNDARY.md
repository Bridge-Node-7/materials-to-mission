# Patent and Intellectual-Property Boundary

This repository contains no third-party claim charts, licensing analysis,
freedom-to-operate opinion, patent-sensitive chemistry, unpublished invention,
or private commercialization terms.

Patent documents may be evidence inputs in a private case, but a patent does not
prove ownership by Bridge Node 7, freedom to operate, technical performance,
qualification, or commercial readiness. Professional legal review remains
necessary where rights affect a real decision.
