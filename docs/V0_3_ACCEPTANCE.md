# v0.3 Public Visual Experience Acceptance

## Foundation Acceptance

The public visual foundation is acceptable only when all of the following are true:

- canonical v0.1.0 schemas remain byte-unchanged;
- Python semantic validation remains authoritative;
- the public view contract is explicitly derived and non-authoritative;
- the browser has no write-back path into canonical records;
- no account, telemetry, hosted backend, or remote persistence is required;
- no composite readiness or risk score is introduced;
- human consequential authority is explicit;
- `FIELD → FOCUS → PROOF` is preserved;
- Explore and Examine modes are represented in controlled design tokens;
- reduced-motion and keyboard-access requirements are explicit;
- no Gallium production snapshot is introduced before DATA-00;
- no real Case 001 or M1 claim is introduced.

## Golden-Journey Acceptance

The first complete browser journey must support:

Arrival → Materials Atlas → Gallium → Trace to Mission → Show Support → Examine →
Decision Room.

The journey must explain:

1. what is claimed;
2. what the evidence supports;
3. what it does not establish;
4. which rules or profile produced the displayed state;
5. what remains unresolved;
6. what proof comes next;
7. who owns the consequential decision.

## Visual Restraint

The initial public experience must not depend on glass-heavy surfaces, neon effects,
particles, 3D globe theater, risk gauges, readiness percentages, constant motion, or
decorative complexity. Confidence comes from provenance, hierarchy, legibility, and
controlled transitions.

## Release Boundary

This foundation does not itself authorize a v0.3.0 release. DATA-00, the Golden Journey,
observed browser UAT, deployment readback, and final release gates remain separate.
