# Decision Passport

The Decision Passport is the concise human-facing output derived from a Material
Assurance Record.

It must make the following visible:

- Decision and mission consequence
- Supported, unknown, contradicted, unsupported, and expired evidence
- Governing weak link
- Triggered critical conditions
- Options and trade-offs
- Recommendation and limitations
- Human decision owner and disposition authority
- Next action, acceptance criteria, success signal, stop rule, and reassessment trigger

The tool may render a passport. It may not approve the conclusion.
