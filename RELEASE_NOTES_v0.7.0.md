# Materials-to-Mission v0.7.0 — Selected Pathways

## Purpose
v0.7.0 improves **discoverability without increasing evidence claims**. The Atlas remains the calm field-level surface; deeper intelligence becomes available only when the user chooses to descend.

## Experience
- `Materials-to-Mission Atlas` remains a one-line identity at release-tested desktop widths.
- Every selected material exposes four optional layers: Connections, Material systems, Evidence basis, and Pathway & proof.
- A compact **Selected pathways** index presents exactly two public deeper examples:
  - **Gallium** — critical mineral / reviewed pathway.
  - **Yttrium Iron Garnet (YIG)** — engineered material system / reviewed context.
- Gallium opens intentionally into Trace / Evidence Horizon / next proof.
- YIG opens intentionally into its Source-to-Mission reviewed context.
- Material Systems and Evidence & Sources remain available without competing with the primary journey.

## Design discipline
The release uses progressive disclosure, recognition over recall, strong information scent, low-choice navigation, and restrained visual hierarchy. Golden-ratio awareness is treated as a balance reference rather than a forced numerical redesign; the accepted visual composition is preserved instead of being remodeled after UAT approval.

## UAT and assurance

## Preserved evidence boundaries
- Exact USGS 2025 60-mineral field and 15 rare-earth count.
- Frozen GA-001 v1.0.0, MF-001, and YIG-001 evidence.
- YIG remains an engineered material system, not a USGS critical mineral.
- Unknown remains non-favorable; Evidence Horizon remains explicit.
- Human consequential authority remains required.
- M0 remains M0.
- No M1, qualification, certification, acquisition approval, mission readiness, supplier/commercial validation, or autonomous consequential-decision claim is made.
